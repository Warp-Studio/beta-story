Questions

Things People Ask Us

How long does it take to get my website? +
Starter plans are typically delivered in 48–72 hours. Pro plans in 24–48 hours. Premium plans are delivered in 24 hours with priority support. We'll confirm your timeline via WhatsApp after your order.
Can I get a custom domain like preetiwedsashutosh.com? +
Yes! Custom domains are included in the Pro and Premium plans. We handle the domain setup for you — all you need to do is choose a name you love. Domain registration charges (typically ₹800–₹1,200/year) are included in the plan price.
What if I need changes after the site is live? +
Starter includes 1 round of edits. Pro includes 3 rounds. Premium includes unlimited edits until your wedding day. Just message us on WhatsApp and we'll update it within a few hours.
How long will my website stay live? +
Your website stays live for 12 months from the date of your wedding. After that, you can renew hosting at a small annual fee if you'd like to keep it as a memento.
Do you work with all wedding budgets and religions? +
Absolutely. We've built sites for Hindu, Muslim, Sikh, Christian, and inter-faith weddings across India and abroad. We celebrate all love stories with equal joy and care.
How do I pay? +
We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), bank transfer, and all major debit/credit cards. Payment is 100% upfront, and we'll send you a proper invoice.
